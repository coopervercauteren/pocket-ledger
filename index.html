How the projection works

Anything marked Repeats monthly that showed up in an earlier month but hasn't been logged yet this month is added on its usual day. Everyday spending (non-repeating expenses) is projected at your daily pace so far, blended with your average daily pace from previous months so a quiet first week doesn't skew it. Non-repeating income isn't projected.

Add a transaction

Upload a CSV exported from your bank. It needs a date column, a description column, and either one amount column (negative = money out) or separate debit and credit columns. Each row is categorized automatically; you can change any category afterwards.
